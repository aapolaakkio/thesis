\section{Literature Review}
\label{sec:literature-review}

The motivation of this thesis comes from the fact that solving the matching and the scheduling together can be expensive and time-consuming, and a solution is not good if either the customer or the driver doesn't accept it. This section reviews the history of the ride-sharing problem, how the problem has been formulated and solved before, and also what is known about the people and their behaviour that the models try to capture.
